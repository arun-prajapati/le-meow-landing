From the first booking to repeat demand

The growth plan starts with services customers already need and expands into categories where they come back.

  1. Acquire

    Use offers, referrals, AI blogs and coordinated social publishing.

  2. Deliver

    Make prices and availability clear and deliver the booked service.

  3. Retain

    Offer packages, renewals, gifting and relevant discovery.

  4. Expand

    Add categories and partners where demand is proven.

Proposed measures include booking conversion, completed appointments, repeat purchases, contribution and acquisition cost.

AI roadmap and research

The October 2026 plan proposes a staged rollout through 2027. The dates depend on available APIs, engineering capacity, QA and pilot results.

Customer and staff tools

Explore recommendations, couples’ itineraries, helper shortlists and children’s learning plans. The plan includes coaching, Buddy Push, trainer feedback, catalogue setup and reports.

AI content and publishing

Use approved catalogue and brand information to draft blogs and campaigns, link to services, schedule supported web and Meta publishing, and review engagement.

AI agents and scam alerts

Plan and screen requests, carry out approved tasks, verify results and escalate when appropriate. Proposed scam checks flag suspicious links, profiles and payment requests.

Le Meow AI Lab

Prototype cross-service planning, supervised software changes, diagnostics and recovery in test environments. Evaluate results and failure modes before piloting.
